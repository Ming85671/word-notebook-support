常见问题

我的词条保存在哪里？

当前版本将词条、笔记和复习计划保存在你的设备本地，不需要账户。

如何删除词条或全部本地数据？

你可以在应用中删除单个词条。删除应用通常会移除设备上的本地应用数据；设备备份由你的 Apple 账户和 iOS 设置管理。

为什么有些词条没有自动释义？

当前版本优先使用内置离线词典。少见词、拼写形式或词典中不存在的词条可能需要你手动补充内容。

Frequently asked questions

Where are my words stored?

The current release stores words, notes, and review schedules locally on your device. No account is required.

How do I delete a word or my local data?

You can delete individual words in the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local data from your device; device backups are controlled by your Apple account and iOS settings.

Why does a word not have an automatic definition?

The current release looks up bundled offline dictionaries first. Rare words, spelling variants, or words not present in those dictionaries may need to be completed manually.
