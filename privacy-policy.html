Word Notebook 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日

概述

Word Notebook 是一款面向英语和韩语学习者的离线生词复习应用。本政策说明当前离线版本如何处理信息。

我们不收集的信息

当前版本不会收集、传输、出售或与第三方共享你的个人信息或学习内容。应用不提供账户注册、云端同步、广告、分析统计、跨应用追踪或已启用的在线 AI/词典服务。

应用不会请求相机、麦克风、照片、通讯录或位置权限。

存储在设备上的内容

你添加或编辑的词条、释义、例句、笔记、学习状态和复习计划仅保存在你的设备上，用于应用的核心功能。内置词典数据同样随应用保存在设备本地。

你的控制权

你可在应用内编辑或删除单个词条。删除应用通常会移除设备上的本地应用数据；如你启用了 iOS 设备备份，备份的保留和删除由你的 Apple 账户及 iOS 备份设置控制。

儿童隐私

当前版本不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童的信息。

政策更新

若未来新增账户、云同步、分析、广告、AI 或其他在线服务，我们会在启用前更新本政策和 App Store 中的隐私说明。更新后的政策会在本页面显示新的生效日期。

Word Notebook Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Overview

Word Notebook is an offline vocabulary-review app for learners of English and Korean. This policy explains how the current offline release handles information.

Information we do not collect

The current release does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information or study content with third parties. It has no account registration, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or enabled online AI or dictionary service.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Information stored on your device

Words, meanings, examples, notes, learning status, and review schedules that you add or edit are stored only on your device to provide the app’s core functions. Bundled dictionary data is also stored locally on your device.

Your choices

You can edit or delete individual words in the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local data from your device. If you use iOS device backups, retention and deletion of backup data are controlled by your Apple account and iOS backup settings.

Children’s privacy

The current release does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, AI, or another online service, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before enabling that feature. The updated policy will show a new effective date on this page.
